Deleting your data

Last updated 26 November 2026

Short version: in the game, open Settings → Support → Delete my data. Your Bevoland account, progress and score are deleted from our servers straight away, and your name leaves the leaderboards. No longer have the game? Email us and we will do it for you.

1. In the game

  1. Open Bevoland and tap Settings.
  2. Under Support, tap Delete my data.
  3. Confirm. The game needs an internet connection for this.

The game then restarts as if it had just been installed.

2. Without the game

Write to support@shortandpetit.com with the subject “Bevoland — delete my data”, and tell us the nickname shown for you on the leaderboards and the kind of device you played on. We may ask a question to make sure the account is yours before deleting it, and we confirm by email once it is done, within 30 days at the latest.

3. What is deleted

  • Your game account and its player ID, including its link to Game Center
  • Your game save: heroes and their levels, coins, gems, missions and settings
  • Your score, and your entries on the all-time and weekly leaderboards. Your name disappears from them immediately, and the emptied entries are removed for good within 30 days.
  • Your friends, and the invitations you sent or received. You disappear from your friends' lists.
  • The game analytics recorded about you, if you had allowed them: the recording stops and Unity, which keeps them for us, deletes them within 30 days. The game then asks you again, or leaves them off until you turn them back on in Settings → Support → Game analytics.
  • The copy of your save on the device (when you delete from the game)

This cannot be undone. Gems, coins and heroes you earned or bought are lost, and we cannot restore them.

4. What is not deleted

  • Your purchases at Apple or Google. They are recorded by the store, not by us. Gems and packs already credited do not come back.
  • Data held by advertising partners. We do not hold it. Change your ad choices in Settings → Support → Ad choices, reset your device's advertising identifier in its settings, or contact the partner through the policy linked in section 4 of the Privacy Policy.
  • Support emails you sent us, kept as described in section 8 of the Privacy Policy. Ask in your message and we will delete them too.
  • Your Game Center or Google Play Games account. It belongs to Apple or Google, not to Bevoland.

Everything Bevoland stores, and why, is listed in the Privacy Policy.
