Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Short version: Bevoland creates a game account for you with Unity Gaming Services, so your progress and your score are kept online. Your Game Center or Google Play Games nickname, your score and the hero you play appear on public leaderboards. The game is free and shows ads. Ads are personalised only if you agree, and on iPhone only if you also allow tracking. The game has no analytics and no crash reporting, and never asks for your real name, email or phone number. You can delete everything at any time in Settings → Support → Delete my data.

The detail below explains every place where data is involved, so that you can check it for yourself.

2. Your game account

The first time you open the Game, it creates an account for you with Unity Gaming Services, the online service provided by Unity Technologies that runs the Game's server. You do not sign up and do not give us an email address or a password. The account is identified by a random player ID.

The account holds:

3. Data stored on your device

The Game also keeps a copy of your save and your settings (language, sound, vibration, controls, graphics quality) on your device, using the standard storage your operating system provides to apps. Uninstalling the Game removes this copy, but not your online account. To delete that, use Delete my data as described in section 10.

4. Advertising

Bevoland is free to play and is paid for by ads: the Game shows a full-screen ad between some matches. Ads are delivered by our ad mediation provider and the ad networks it works with (our “advertising partners”). The Game asks for your choices before any ad is loaded.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game shows a consent form the first time you play. It lists our advertising partners and lets you accept or refuse personalised advertising, for each partner and each purpose if you wish.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the Game also asks for permission to track you through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your device's advertising identifier is not shared with anyone.
  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad choices. You can also reset or delete your device's advertising identifier, or turn tracking off for every app, in your device's own settings.
  • Buying No Ads in the shop stops the Game from showing ads.

What our advertising partners receive

To show an ad, our advertising partners receive from your device:

  • Technical information: device model, operating system and version, language, screen size, network type, and the name and version of the Game
  • Your IP address, from which they derive an approximate location (country or city, never your precise location)
  • How you interact with ads: which ads were shown, whether you tapped them or closed them
  • Only if you agreed to personalised ads (and, on iPhone, allowed tracking): your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)

If you agree, partners may use this data to show you ads based on your interests and on what you do in other apps and websites. If you refuse, you still see ads, but they are not based on a profile of you. Partners then use the data only to deliver an ad, limit how often you see the same one, measure that it was shown and prevent fraud.

Legal basis Personalised ads, and the storage of or access to an advertising identifier on your device: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Ad choices. Non-personalised ads: our legitimate interest in keeping the Game free (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), limited to what is needed to deliver an ad.
Who decides what We choose which partners the Game works with. Each advertising partner decides how it uses the data it collects, and is responsible for that under its own privacy policy. The consent form links each partner's policy.

We do not receive your advertising identifier, and we do not combine ad data with your game account. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Purchases

The Game offers optional purchases: gem packs, the Starter Pack, the League pack and No Ads. These transactions are processed entirely by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google), depending on where you obtained the Game.

We never see, receive or store your payment details. We do not receive your name, billing address, card number or any part of it. The Game receives only a confirmation from the platform that a purchase completed, which it uses to credit what you bought. A record of one-time purchases (such as No Ads) is kept in your game save, so that they are not credited twice. Settings → Support → Purchases → Restore asks the platform which purchases your store account owns.

Your purchase is governed by the privacy policy of the platform you bought it from:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

6. Game Center and Google Play Games

The Game signs you in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android) if you use them, to read the nickname shown on the leaderboards and, on iOS, to link your account as described in section 2. You can play without either. Your use of these services is governed by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies, linked in section 5.

8. What the Game does not do

For the avoidance of doubt, Bevoland contains:

  • No sign-up with an email address or password
  • No analytics and no crash reporting
  • No chat or messaging between players
  • No social-media integrations or share trackers
  • No access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos
  • No sale of personal data to anyone, for any purpose

Network access is used for the Game's online account and leaderboards (section 2), for ads (section 4) and for the platform purchase flow (section 5).

9. Children

Bevoland is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 15 in France), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the Game, you can delete the child's account and data with Settings → Support → Delete my data on their device, or write to us and we will delete it. The same applies to a support email a child may have sent us.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your personal data, and to object to its processing. Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time, without affecting what was done before. You may exercise these rights at any time by writing to support@shortandpetit.com.

Two of them you can exercise yourself, directly in the Game:

  • Erasure: Settings → Support → Delete my data deletes your account, your save and your score from our servers, removes your name from the leaderboards, and resets the Game on your device. It cannot be undone and needs an internet connection. See Deleting your data for the detail, and for how to ask us if you no longer have the Game.
  • Withdrawing ad consent: Settings → Ad choices, as described in section 4.

For data collected by an advertising partner, you can also contact that partner directly, using the policy linked in the consent form.

If you consider that your data protection rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the French supervisory authority, the Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés (CNIL), www.cnil.fr, or with the authority of your country of residence.

11. International transfers

Unity Gaming Services and some of our advertising partners process data in the United States and other countries outside the European Union. Where they do, the transfer is covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for companies certified under it, or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google under their own policies and safeguards, which are linked in section 5.
