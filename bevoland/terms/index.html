7. Chests

Some Virtual Items are given at random. To be clear about the odds before you spend anything:

  • Hero chests (Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary) are bought with coins in the shop, or received as rewards. A chest always gives one hero of its own rarity: a Legendary chest gives a Legendary hero. The hero is chosen with equal chance among the heroes of that rarity you do not own yet, so a chest never gives a hero you already have. For example, if 4 Epic heroes are missing from your collection, each has a 1 in 4 chance of coming out of an Epic chest.
  • The shop stops selling a chest once you own every hero of its rarity. A chest received as a reward for a rarity you have completed gives 25% of that chest's coin price instead.
  • Victory chests, received free after a won match, may contain a new hero. They cannot be bought.
