Bevoland

Policy documents

Bevoland is a free arena game for iOS and Android, animals against humans. Your progress and score are kept in an online game account, your nickname appears on the leaderboards, and the game shows ads that are personalised only if you agree. It records how it is played only with your agreement, and you can delete everything from inside the game.

  • Privacy Policy Your game account, the leaderboards, ads, game analytics, and your rights under the GDPR.
  • Terms & Conditions Licence to play, fair play, virtual items, chest odds, ads, purchases and refunds.
  • Deleting your data How to delete your account and progress, in the game or by email.

Support

Something broken, or a question about any of these documents? Write to support@shortandpetit.com. The game's Settings → Support → Contact us button opens a message that already includes the game's version and your device model, which makes problems much faster to reproduce.
